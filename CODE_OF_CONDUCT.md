# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want i18n Autopilot to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Showing empathy and kindness toward others

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing others' private information without their permission
- Trolling, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional
  setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at
**banjolakunri@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues or other
contributions that break this code of conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone who does so.
